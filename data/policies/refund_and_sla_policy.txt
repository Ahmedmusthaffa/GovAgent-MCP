COMPANY OPERATING POLICY: CUSTOMER DISPUTES, REFUNDS & SLA TERMS

1. DISPUTE & REFUND ELIGIBILITY:
   - Any customer experiencing delivery delays or damaged shipments may request a refund within 14 calendar days of order creation.
   - For orders under $500.00 USD, customer support specialists and interns are authorized to process standard compensation or initiate product re-shipment.
   - For orders exceeding $500.00 USD, explicit Manager or Executive approval is mandatory before any financial refund transaction is dispatched.

2. SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENTS (SLA):
   - Enterprise Tier Accounts: Guaranteed response time within 4 hours. Priority dispatch applies.
   - Growth & Standard Tier Accounts: Standard response window within 24 hours.

3. DISPUTE RESOLUTION PROTOCOL:
   - Step 1: Verify order status in the central database.
   - Step 2: Confirm refund eligibility against the $500 threshold.
   - Step 3: Issue official resolution notice to the registered customer contact email.
